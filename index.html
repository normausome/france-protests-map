France unrest, Sept–Oct 2026: incidents named by news outlets

Loading incidents…

  • The movement began as a high-school (lycéen) protest at Lycée Saint-Exupéry in Créteil on 17–18 Sept over teacher shortages and Parcoursup, and went national from 28 Sept. (Guardian, BBC)
  • On the national day of action, Sat 17 Oct, about 550,000 marched per the Interior Ministry (110,000 in Paris); the CGT says 800,000. (Guardian, BBC)
  • Latest official national tally: the Interior Ministry counted 2,397 interpellations on 17 Oct and 997 police and gendarmes injured, 7 seriously. (RTL, RFI, BFMTV/AFP)
  • Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin said on TF1 on 19 Oct that 2,513 people had been held in custody (gardes à vue) since Saturday and 67 jailed. (Le Parisien live)
  • About 3,000 businesses were looted or vandalised according to PM Lecornu; CCI France says about 2,000 were directly hit. Commerce Minister Serge Papin says "at least 3,000" were damaged or looted over the weekend. (NBC, BBC live, Le Figaro live)
  • First local curfews, Mon 19 Oct: Paris 23:00–06:00 around place de la République and the streets to Les Halles; Lyon hyper-centre and Presqu'île 21:00–06:00; Nice, minors in the city centre 21:00–06:00; Lille, Douai, Wattignies and Denain, unaccompanied minors 20:00–06:00. (Le Parisien live)
  • Next days of action: Thu 22 Oct, high-school students (the Coordination nationale lycéenne calls lycéens out on 22 and 24 Oct); Sat 24 Oct, gilets jaunes "Acte II"; 5 Nov, CGT day of strikes and demonstrations. (Le Figaro live, Le Parisien live)
